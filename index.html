Smartfon Connect App

To elevate our customer experience and streamline service center operations, SMARTFON STUDIO SP Z O O maintains a dedicated mobile application.

The Smartfon Connect app allows our retail and repair customers to instantly track the status of their devices in our workshop, purchase extended insurance policies, and browse our latest electronics catalog directly from their Android devices.

  • Live Repair Tracker: Monitor diagnostics, parts ordering, and fixes.
  • Digital Device Insurance: Manage your tech coverage policies.
  • Retail E-Commerce: Browse and order smart accessories.
  • Equipment Rental Booking: Reserve fleet or tech hardware.
